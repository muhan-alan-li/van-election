# Parks and schools: quick source review

Checked October 14, 2026. This is a limited discovery pass, not a complete review
of all 35 Park Board and 31 school trustee candidates. Sources below contain
campaign promises and candidate statements; historical claims and delivery costs
have not been independently checked. The initial discovery notes below are retained
for reference. The subsequent profile update is described at the end.

## Findings

Useful material is readily available. School and parks platforms need not be
inferred from a party's general housing or policing policies. Shared plans should
be labelled as shared plans, and individual responses attributed to their author.

### Individual Park Board responses

[Vancouver Public Space Network's 2026 candidate survey](https://vancouverpublicspace.ca/our-work/2026-park-board-candidate-survey/)
lists 16 candidate responses as of its October 10 update. Topics include facilities,
climate adaptation, funding, elected-board governance, reconciliation and access.
This is a useful collection of candidate statements, rather than an independent
assessment of their promises.

Responses are listed for ballot numbers 200, 202, 206, 210, 211, 213, 214, 215,
216, 217, 221, 222, 226, 229, 230 and 231. Only two responses were sampled in this
pass; the other 14 are discovery leads, not reviewed profiles. Some published
names differ from our roster spelling, so identify records by ballot number and
office before merging.

- [Tom Digby, #222](https://vancouverpublicspace.ca/our-work/2026-park-board-candidate-survey/tom-digby-222/):
  discusses a new 50-metre pool at Hastings or Kensington, tree planting, air
  filtration in recreation facilities, borrowing for infrastructure, and preserving
  the elected Park Board. These are his published survey statements; numerical
  claims about past budgets and debt have not been verified.
- [Tyler Petersen, #214](https://vancouverpublicspace.ca/our-work/2026-park-board-candidate-survey/tyler-petersen-214-onecity/):
  proposes better lighting at Andy Livingstone Park, cooling and air filtration in
  community facilities, a 40% tree-canopy target, and retaining earned parks revenue.
  Supports preserving the elected board and preparing projects for external grants.

### OneCity: dedicated shared plans

[Better Schools, September 29](https://www.onecityvancouver.ca/news/better-schools)
explicitly names Krista Sigurdson, Rory Brown, Sherry Breshears, Christopher Lee and
Steve Cardwell as its School Board team. Proposes retaining school land in public
ownership, redeveloping Kingsgate and Britannia as community hubs, public/non-profit
childcare, universal school meals, protected school programs, and more open board
meetings. This is a shared school platform, not five separately researched
individual platforms.

[Parks, Pipes & Pools, September 29](https://www.onecityvancouver.ca/news/parks-pipes-pools)
names John Irwin, Tyler Petersen and Dominic Denofrio as its Park Board team.
Proposes keeping earned revenue with the board, a parks gift-shop program, a 40%
tree-canopy target, more parkland, restoration of buried creeks, lifeguard staffing,
and new pools at Mount Pleasant, Hastings and Marpole. This is a shared parks plan.

### Vancouver Liberals: separate school and parks sections

[Full 2026 platform](https://www.vancouverliberals.com/platform) has dedicated
Park Board and School Board sections.

- Parks: preserve the elected board; propose $1.35 billion in recreation investment;
  renew pools and community centres; expand washrooms and low-income access; remove
  beach/community-centre parking fees. Funding feasibility has not been checked.
- Schools: prioritize seismic upgrades and overcrowding; retain school land in public
  ownership; add childcare; protect learning supports and meals; retain the reformed
  School Liaison Officer program; introduce AI rules. Treat these as shared commitments.

The parking commitment also means our existing parking question's ABC-only mapping
is incomplete. This discovery pass does not change that mapping.

### COPE: candidate-specific priorities on its team page

[COPE team page](https://www.votecope.ca/the-cope-team) provides attributable priorities,
although detail varies:

- School trustees: Derrick O'Keefe supports universal meals, accessible childcare and
  public-benefit land use; Melanie Cheng emphasizes protecting school land and parent
  participation; Gwen Giesbrecht emphasizes meals and public land. Suzie Mah's entry
  mainly describes her claimed record, which needs separate verification.
- Park Board: Jamie Han prioritizes cycling infrastructure and recreation investment;
  Tamiko Suzuki prioritizes neglected parks, tree canopy and ecosystem restoration;
  Cease Wyss and Sacia Burton describe sustainable parks and affordable recreation.

These entries support short candidate summaries, not a comprehensive account of
each platform. Campaign claims about opponents should not become guide facts.

### ABC: platform lead, not detailed school research

[September 21 platform announcement](https://abcvancouver.ca/2026/09/abc-vancouver-launches-2026-platform-lets-keep-vancouver-moving-forward/)
explicitly says the platform covers Council, Park Board and School Board, and
announces a family-policy release for September 28. The announcement alone does
not provide enough school-specific detail to populate trustee positions. The
family release and full platform remain follow-up leads.

## Reasonable next use

Start with the two parties' dedicated shared school/parks plans and the published
candidate survey. Keep individual commitments distinct from slate commitments.
Retain unknown positions where sources do not establish an answer. Before using
school questions, find explicit positions that create meaningful choices; universal
support for meals or safe buildings alone offers little differentiation.

Stop this research pass here. Other parties, remaining survey responses, social
accounts and nomination documents have not been exhaustively searched. Absence
from these notes means unchecked, not that a candidate lacks a platform.
