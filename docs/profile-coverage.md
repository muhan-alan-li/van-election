## Sources incorporated

- [Side Street candidate replies](https://sidestreetvancouver.ca/election/candidate-responses/):
  personal replies for Amauri Caliman and seven councillors; TrueBlue's reply is
  retained as shared council policy. The COPE candidate average is not used as an
  official party response.
- [Vote4Tomorrow](https://vote4tomorrow.ca/election-2026/vancouver/): all 60 published
  respondent pages identified in its policy table, including the 17 policy
  selections and selected written local answers. Publisher scores and endorsements
  are not imported. The explicitly duplicated Liberal reply is labelled shared.
  Neutral selections remain neutral; absent written answers supply no position.
- [Smart Density](https://smartdensityvancouver.ca/candidates/): seven personal PDF
  responses and six party PDF responses. Personal and party replies retain their
  respective scope. Only respondents matching the final ballot roster are used;
  pre-nomination candidate listings do not expand the roster.
- [The Griffins' Nest, week 1](https://www.ehnewspaper.ca/articles/candidates-questionnaire-week-1-meet-your-school-board-candidates-and-find-out-why-theyre-running),
  [week 2](https://www.ehnewspaper.ca/articles/candidates-questionnaire-week-2-generative-ai-in-schools),
  and [week 3](https://www.ehnewspaper.ca/articles/candidates-questionnaire-week-3-specialized-district-programs):
  personal school priorities, AI positions and specialized-program answers.
  Only published answers are included, not names in the non-response lists.
- [Women Transforming Cities pledge results](https://www.womentransformingcities.org/blog/our-city-hall-pledge):
  individual commitments checked against the published result images. ABC's decision
  not to sign is not treated as opposing the six policy goals. TEAM's two abstentions
  are preserved, and blank result cells do not become commitments.
- Existing reviewed campaign platforms, dedicated board plans, COPE candidate
  priorities and the 16 VPSN Park Board responses described in
  [the earlier board research notes](parks-schools-research.md).

## Recordings and attribution

[UBC's September 8 housing debate](https://bsh.ubc.ca/vancouver-mayoral-debate/)
names Kareem Allam, Rebecca Bligh, Pete Fry and Colleen Hardwick and links the
recording. Their profiles include that viewing link.

[WTC's council forum](https://www.womentransformingcities.org/ourcityhall) links a
recording featuring selected party representatives. Council profiles include the
forum as viewing context, without claiming each candidate participated.

Neither recording exposed a usable transcript through the research tools. No
policy statements, quotes or timestamps are invented from these videos.

## Interpretation limits

Profiles summarize published commitments. Review does not establish that a policy
is funded, deliverable or already implemented. Campaign claims about past records
or opponents are not converted into verified facts. Shared-only profiles say so.

City-wide climate positions stated by Park Board or school candidates are labelled
as advocacy rather than powers they could exercise through those boards.
Contradictory or tentative answers are retained where material, including Blair
Coleman's written rejection of climate change alongside his policy checklist,
and Alex Tkach's requests for further research.

This update enriches candidate profiles and their source links. It does not add
questions, change candidate ranking, or infer new finder matches from these records.
